# Retention and privacy decisions — approval required

`M10A-retention-matrix.json` records proposed engineering handling, accountable roles and open approval for every requested record domain. It is not an approved legal retention schedule. No destructive retention job is activated. Business/Security Owner and qualified privacy/accounting reviewers must approve periods, contractual holds, deletion authority and provider settings before production.

Archive is the default reversible business action. Payment, agreement, audit and inventory movement records require integrity-preserving corrections rather than blind deletion. A privacy request starts with independently verified identity/authority, a restricted case ID, scoped relationship discovery and hold review. Export only the authorized person's scope using a secure destination; never expose third-party business data. Separate profile/contact redaction from required transaction/audit provenance. Obtain approval for any irreversible anonymization/deletion, document stable IDs/counts and actor/reason, and reconcile relationships, balances, inventory and analytics afterward. Suppression cannot simply be erased if that would resume unwanted communications; determine the minimal retained suppression proof under approved policy. No public bulk-export/deletion endpoint is introduced.

Backups expire through a controlled lifecycle; deletion from the live database does not remove immutable historical backups immediately. Maintain a restricted deletion ledger so restored records do not silently undo an approved deletion. Before reactivation, replay approved redactions and revoke restored authentication artifacts. Restrictions/holds apply to incident evidence and backups, with independently tested access and expiry. Real incident store/ACL, approved log sink, staffed alert destination and human contacts are not yet configured.

Provider review (official documentation retrieved 2026-09-16; account-specific configuration not verified):

- [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) distinguish application state from abuse monitoring; `store:false` does not by itself establish Zero Data Retention. Default abuse-monitoring content retention can be up to 30 days, with exceptions and approval-based controls. Glara must verify the actual organization/project agreement and endpoint configuration. No ZDR approval is claimed.
- [Resend retention](https://resend.com/security/gdpr) states 30-day email/log retention for Free/Pro/Scale, flexible Enterprise retention, and separate backup/account termination timelines. Verify the actual plan, DPA, access permissions and any early-deletion process before production; application deletion alone does not erase the provider's copy.
- [Convex backups](https://docs.convex.dev/database/backup-restore) have documented retention dependent on manual/daily/weekly mode and plan. Code, environment variables and scheduled functions are outside data snapshots. Verify chosen region/plan/contract, configuration recovery and independent encrypted retention against approved policy. No residency or compliance guarantee is asserted.
- Vercel/log vendor: actual project/log destination and contractual retention are unconfigured; collect exact plan, access, expiry and deletion evidence during authorized setup. Google Calendar remains disabled/deferred; review its data handling before any activation.

Engineering review is complete only for the stated documentary scope. Provider-account verification and business/legal policy approval remain open in the canonical readiness register.

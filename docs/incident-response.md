# Glara OS incident response

Scope: M10A Sections 79–130. **DETECT → CONTAIN → PRESERVE → ASSESS → RECOVER → VERIFY → LEARN.** This is an operator runbook, not an application endpoint. Commands below require the legitimate platform operator or an authenticated application role; they do not confer access. M10A exercises use isolated local fixtures only. Production actions require separately authorized incident authority and the verified production environment. M10B remains unauthorized.

## First ten minutes

1. Declare without waiting for complete root cause. Copy the empty [incident register template](incident-register.template.json) into the restricted incident workspace; assign `INC-YYYY-NNNN` (prefix drills `DRILL-`). Record detection/declaration UTC, reporter, environment/deployment, categories, severity, modules, symptoms and containment state. Separate confirmed facts, hypotheses and unknowns.
2. Assign Incident Commander (IC), Technical Lead (TL), Security/Privacy Owner (SPO), Business Owner (BO) and a recorder. One named human can hold several roles; record each responsibility. Contact details come from the restricted [contact register](emergency-contacts.template.json), never invented contacts.
3. For SEV-0 freeze Go-Live/releases and begin containment immediately. For SEV-1 urgently contain and freeze affected changes. Prefer narrow containment; preserve safe read access. If active harm prevents collecting evidence first, contain now and record why.
4. Verify exact platform account, project, deployment and frontend origin before any change. Record the current deployed SHA from deployment evidence, not just the local Git HEAD. The only known development deployment is `woozy-jaguar-392`; there is no approved production ID in this repository. Missing/ambiguous identity means STOP, not use the default CLI target.
5. Preserve record/audit/provider event IDs, UTC timestamps, deployment SHA and sanitized configuration states. Copy only necessary evidence to restricted storage, with source, collector, timestamp and SHA-256 digest. Keep originals read-only. Do not delete users, messages, queues, ledgers, AI threads or execution history as containment.
6. Apply the relevant controls below. Record authenticated operator/audit reference, time, reason, affected capability, previous state and verified resulting state. Never put secret values in chat, Git, tickets, screenshots, AI prompts or reports; use secret name plus rotation status.
7. IC publishes an internal update every **15 minutes for SEV-0**, **30 minutes for SEV-1** until contained, then explicitly agrees a cadence. SEV-2 same-business-day investigation where practical; SEV-3 normal engineering. These are response targets, not claims of staffed 24/7 coverage. Escalate unacknowledged ownership through BO's private contact tree.

Priority: protect people/data; prevent further unauthorized activity; preserve trustworthy evidence and authoritative business truth; restore safe service; determine causes; prevent recurrence. Avoid destructive improvisation.

## Severity and authority

| Severity       | Evidence/impact                                                                                                                                                 | Response                                                                               |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SEV-0 critical | Active credential compromise, privileged takeover, broad exfiltration, uncontrolled financial mutation, widespread unauthorized sends or destructive corruption | Immediate containment; freeze Go-Live/changes; IC + TL + SPO + BO                      |
| SEV-1 high     | Limited unauthorized access, duplicate real sends, serious authorization bypass, material recoverable corruption                                                | Urgent containment/investigation; SPO assesses privacy; BO assesses operational impact |
| SEV-2 medium   | Provider outage, bounded workflow fault or inconsistency with safe fallback                                                                                     | Same-business-day investigation where practical; elevate if impact expands             |
| SEV-3 low      | Minor non-security UI/operational defect                                                                                                                        | Normal engineering process; no unnecessary incident cadence                            |

Controlled multi-select categories: `security`, `privacy`, `data_integrity`, `availability`, `communication`, `ai`, `automation`, `external_integration`. Severity can change with new evidence; append the decision and rationale, do not rewrite the initial assessment.

IC coordinates decisions and authorizes phased restoration with BO; TL diagnoses and executes approved technical actions; SPO assesses access/data exposure and obtains privacy/legal advice; BO determines business/customer impact and approves external statements with the appropriate privacy/legal reviewer. Neither M8 nor M9 automatically communicates an incident externally. AI drafting, if separately permitted and safe, is not approval to send.

## Emergency controls: supported paths and limits

Use existing authenticated settings and trusted platform secret configuration. There is **no public global kill-switch endpoint** and this milestone adds none. App settings use server role checks, version checks and server timestamps. Preserve settings before changing only the required fields; on a version conflict re-read, do not force overwrite. A capability is not contained until its observed behavior has been verified.

Production examples are deliberately absent until its environment identity and incident authority are approved. Environment edits are not automatically app audit records: attach platform audit evidence to the private timeline. Existing settings audits do not all contain an incident reason; record the linkage externally. Missing attributable platform evidence is an open readiness issue, not permission to invent an actor.

## Account, Owner and unauthorized-access incident

1. Identify affected user IDs and evidence of misuse; classify Owner compromise SEV-0/1 by evidence. Capture profile/role changes, sessions metadata, audit IDs and current assignment scope (Realtors, Opportunities, Projects). Never collect tokens/passwords in the report.
2. Archive only the affected account using trusted internal administration. Preserve the identity and existing audit. Test the compromised identity's existing-session CRM, M8, M9 and sensitive mutation denial. An application Owner session is not authorization to call internal administration functions.
3. Revoke sessions and pending recovery/invitation capabilities using a tested supported auth administration procedure. **That complete operator procedure is not yet implemented/tested here (IR-01); archival is only immediate business-access containment. Keep archived until revocation and credential recovery are independently verified.** Do not assume sign-out on one browser revokes all sessions.
4. Inspect mutations, role/profile changes, invitation/provisioning history, M8 proposals, provider settings, communication approvals/outbox, financial ledgers and source assignments during the exposure window. Map affected IDs and access duration; preserve unauthorized-read uncertainty.
5. For Owner takeover, use a separate pre-enrolled trusted platform operator and BO recovery channel. Independently verify identity through the approved private recovery contact, review platform MFA and active sessions, remove unauthorized roles, rotate affected secrets, then verify authorization before restoration. If no trusted operator/recovery channel exists, escalate to platform recovery; do not create an unauthenticated backdoor. Production MFA/recovery drill remains a blocker.

## Email storm, duplicate send and wrong recipient

1. Disable environment email and pause app sending. Disable affected M7 rules if they generated communication activities; M7 drafts are not authorization to send. Capture changes and inspect already-dispatched requests.
2. Owner/Admin inspects `communications.queueHealth`, `operationsHealth`, `unknownReview`, `deliveryHistory` and protected Communication detail. Record message IDs, send keys (restricted metadata), requester/approver, snapshot version, source/recipient IDs, provider IDs, delivery events, attempt times and statuses. Paginate scans; queue counters marked `partial` are not full totals.
3. Preserve content snapshots in access-controlled evidence only. Determine actual recipient scope and provider delivery facts. For wrong-recipient mail assess included sensitive fields, source, attachments if any, and number of messages. Do not assume provider acceptance proves inbox receipt, or a 5xx proves non-delivery.
4. Inspect request/send-key idempotency, human approval/source rechecks, worker claims and retry state. Unknown delivery stays held; do not set it to ready, clone it, or create a new key to retry. Keep preference/suppression history intact.
5. Run all paginated M9 scans: `communications.reconcilePage`, `outboxReconcilePage`, `eventReconcilePage`, `providerReconcilePage`. Resolve findings against authoritative provider evidence through the existing authorized reconciliation workflow. Read-only queries must not be used as repair authority.
6. Fix and test the cause. IC/TL/SPO/BO review recipients and outcomes. Customer notification needs an explicit human/legal/business decision; nothing auto-sends. Re-enable only with recorded authorization, clean relevant scans, bounded queues and a separately authorized controlled test. Development email must return to disabled after acceptance.

## Secret and webhook compromise

Identify secret by name only; preserve provider audit IDs and usage intervals. Disable the affected consumer, revoke/rotate securely, assess exposure scope, verify controlled recovery and remove obsolete material. Follow [secret inventory/rotation](M10A-environments-and-secrets.md).

- **Resend API:** disable/pause M9; also evaluate `AUTH_RESEND_KEY` separately. Revoke suspected key in the correct provider account, inspect activity/mappings and sender/domain changes, install replacement privately, verify no unauthorized sends or config changes. Keep production sending off until authorized verification.
- **Webhook signing:** preserve suspicious event IDs; rotate current secret and use `_PREVIOUS` only if a provider transition requires a short documented overlap. A known-compromised old secret is not safe merely because overlap is supported: remove it as soon as possible and contain intake while establishing the new boundary. Record overlap deadline/operator, reject obsolete signatures, reconcile recent state against provider truth. Signing-key exposure does not prove API-key exposure. Forged delivery/suppression effects need assessment, not deletion of evidence.
- **Unsubscribe material:** pause dispatch/token issuance, use authenticated Owner/Admin `communications.rotateUnsubscribeGeneration` with current version and incident reason to revoke outstanding generation; rotate `M9_UNSUBSCRIBE_SECRET` privately. Preserve legitimate preferences/suppressions. Newly generated tokens must use the new generation; old links may cease to work, requiring a reviewed preference-support path, not unsolicited replacement mail.
- **OpenAI:** disable M8 settings and security approval, revoke/rotate key, inspect provider usage/cost/retention and potential transmitted context. Preserve request/context/evidence IDs under restricted access. Inspect human-approved proposals and resulting CRM mutations; provider output has no independent mutation authority. Core modules should remain usable. Validate a replacement only with separately approved fictional context, not production data.

## AI and automation malfunction

For unauthorized M8 context, unsafe proposal or consequential hallucination: disable AI, preserve request IDs and restricted context/output evidence, identify the retrieval authorization path and data sent to the provider, inspect human approval/execution and resultant business changes, assess privacy impact. Deleting a conversation is not remediation. Fix retrieval/authorization/validation, run adversarial tests and reconcile actual effects before re-enable.

For runaway M7: disable implicated rules, or all rules for systemic behavior, retaining immutable rule versions, execution/action/queue history. Identify duplicate Activities and downstream M9 drafts or business impact. Reconcile using `automation.preview`, `actions`, `history` and `health`; repairs require explicit privileged review. Do not delete executions or blindly replay jobs. No atomic global M7 freeze is promised; track IR-02.

## Financial, Inventory and analytics integrity

- **M5:** stop affected writes; preserve Invoice state/items, Payment ledger/allocations/reversals, Credits, Damage charges and audit. Compare authoritative transaction events and independent balance calculations. Do not manually edit totals to make reports match. Use existing commercial validation/reconciliation tooling only after its exact scope and read/write behavior are reviewed; this runbook does not invent a universal repair endpoint.
- **M4:** restrict affected asset/stock movements; reconcile physical asset/quantity count with movement ledger, project assignments, reservations, returns, inspections, damage and disposition. Obtain operations confirmation of physical truth. Never overwrite physical state to match a computed total. Preserve original movement evidence and corrections.
- **M6:** source modules remain authoritative. Owner starts `analyticsReconciliation.start`, advances bounded pages with `advance`, reads paginated `result`. Require a stable watermark and zero source/bucket drift. A diagnostic run writes reconciliation evidence, not source corrections. `repairBucket` is Owner-only and source-backed; repair only a proven derived error with reason and fresh source-clean evidence. Source corruption requires a source-module repair. Do not modify M5 from M6 to hide drift.
- After material incidents run applicable M4/M5 independent evidence checks, M6 rebuild, M7 execution review and all M9 scans. Future Calendar only if actually enabled and accepted. Zero **unexplained** drift is required before full confidence restoration. Link every explained/accepted exception to BO/TL ownership; do not label an incomplete scan clean.

## Provider, Convex and frontend outages

Resend/OpenAI/future Calendar outages degrade the affected feature, not business truth. Verify provider status using its authenticated console/status channel and record timestamp; do not assume every error is a provider outage. M9 429 retries are bounded; uncertain 5xx/transport results are held for reconciliation, and repeated failures open the app circuit. Do not replay unknown sends after recovery. Inspect queue age, attempts, circuit reason and partial result indicators. Keep core operations available when safe.

For Convex outage: stop repeated manual mutations, pause external providers through their independent controls if needed, preserve deployment/incident context, use BO-approved offline operational notes in restricted storage. Record intended action, business record ID and time without assuming it committed. After recovery reconcile before controlled re-entry with existing idempotency; never substitute an ad-hoc database. Decide ordering with operations; avoid double-entering payments/movements.

For frontend outage with healthy backend: IC/TL investigate origin/deployment/provider state, consider compatible frontend rollback, use approved internal contingency. Normal staff get no deployment token or direct privileged backend workaround. Frontend unavailability does not prove workers are paused. Provider consoles may remain necessary if the application/backend is unavailable.

## Bad deployment, migration and backup recovery

1. Freeze rollout and subsequent migration steps. Preserve bad deployed SHA, prior known-good deployed SHA, incident UTC, schema/data state and configuration versions. `git rev-parse HEAD` proves only local source; deployment records prove hosted source.
2. TL compares the exact commits, schema definitions, validators, persisted formats, worker leases and migration history. Check old code against current data in an isolated restore. Document preconditions, backup reference, verification, irreversible boundary and rollback versus forward-fix decision. No blind schema reversal.
3. If code rollback is compatible and does not reintroduce a security gap, use the hosting/platform's approved deployment process under separate authorization. If data changes are irreversible or compatibility unproven, hold and forward-fix after backup/verification. Rollback cannot undo external email or a payment already recorded; preserve/reconcile those independently.
4. Restore requires a **tested** backup runbook: verify exact source environment/account/deployment, backup timestamp/checksum, restore target identity, access controls and expected recovery point. Restore to a separately approved isolated target first; disable ALL external workers/communications/AI/acceptance helpers there. Never restore production into ordinary development or vice versa. Validate record counts, relationships, ledgers, auth/roles, audit and reconciliation before any cutover. Do not print/export secrets with evidence.
5. **No restore drill or production restore process has been accepted yet (IR-05).** If the tested runbook is unavailable, stop destructive restore/cutover and escalate TL/platform support. This procedure is a decision guard, not fabricated proof of backup recoverability. Backup/restore and migration readiness remain M10A/M10B blockers.

## Privacy assessment and communication

SPO records data categories, affected people/record counts with uncertainty, unauthorized recipient/access, duration, evidence of actual access, containment and remaining exposure. Assess wrong-recipient messages from the preserved snapshot/provider facts. Obtain appropriate privacy/legal assessment of notification duties; the application must not make legal conclusions. Keep personal details in the restricted annex, not public Git or general status updates.

Each internal update: incident ID/severity/environment; confirmed facts; hypotheses; unknowns; containment actions and evidence; operational fallback; next decision/time/owner. Avoid blame and speculative causes. External notification requires BO/SPO/legal decision, authorized channel, exact approved content and recipients; the existence of an incident never authorizes automatic M9 sends.

## Recovery, re-enable, closure and learning

IC requires evidence, not merely disappearing errors: root cause contained, implicated credentials revoked/rotated, unsafe release removed or corrected, permissions verified, provider state and queues safe, relevant independent reconciliation clean, monitoring stable for an agreed observation window including at least one affected worker/retry cycle. Record actual window and missing monitoring; do not invent production observations.

For EACH capability record disabling actor/time/reason/scope; evidence IDs; re-enable approver/executor/time; exact new state; residual risks; rollback trigger. BO approves business resumption, TL verifies integrity, SPO approves security/privacy boundary. A runbook authorization record is not a software override. Calendar cannot be restored past its deferred M9 Gate. M10A never authorizes production or customer traffic.

Close only when containment is complete, service recovered or an explicitly accepted safe workaround exists, impact is sufficiently understood, evidence retained, required reconciliation completed and follow-ups assigned. Preserve append-oriented timeline; corrections append a reference to the original entry. Use server/provider timestamps where available and separately label operator-recorded UTC; never backdate or rewrite history.

SEV-0/1 and meaningful SEV-2 require a post-incident review: impact, chronology, detection, root/contributing causes, containment/recovery, successes/failures, corrective actions, owners and deadlines. Do not end analysis at “human error”; identify authorization/default/review/monitoring/idempotency/runbook conditions. Review within five business days as an operational target; IC records justified delays. Track corrective actions in the restricted incident register and normal engineering workflow. Unresolved high-risk items block M10B until reviewed/resolved.

## Registers, access and evidence

Templates contain no real incidents or contacts. Copy them to an approved restricted operational store, never a public repository. Restrict actual incident content to IC/TL/SPO/BO and approved responders; enforce ACLs/audit/retention there. `.incidents/` is ignored locally, but Git ignore is **not** access control. Templates are not a new user-visible application module and offer no public privileged API.

The register records declaration, affected systems, containment/recovery, closure/postmortem, append timeline, evidence references, re-enablement and corrective actions. Do not overwrite chronology or preserve unnecessary secret/customer content. Verify evidence file digests with `Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 -LiteralPath <restricted-file>`; preserve original and sanitized versions separately. Before M10B, fill real responder/on-call/provider/privacy contacts, exercise trusted Owner recovery, and establish restricted storage and audit attribution. These are open prerequisites, not completed configuration.

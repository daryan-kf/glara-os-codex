# Performance baseline and limits

The final measured evidence is in M10A-final-results.json. `tests/convex/performance.test.ts` records nine in-process Convex-test operation distributions with ten samples after warm-up, 501 fictional Realtors and small project/Inventory/commercial fixtures. Other scale cases cover 1,000 activities, 206 inventory locations and the existing 1,000 products/3,000 assets/1,000 ledger rows. These validate paging and transaction bounds; they are not production capacity guarantees.

CRM list/search hydrates only visible records. Activity history and contact-date boundaries use indexes; history is cursor-paged at 30 rows, ordered by creation time descending. Follow-ups use bounded cursor pages and permit continuation through sparse filters. Derived sorting has a 2,000-Realtor narrowing error; the existing general CRM scan limit is 10,000. No rows are silently dropped to produce a misleading complete total. Commercial dashboard has an explicit 501-row capacity error; health samples use partial flags.

Inventory categories and locations load successive native pages of at most 100 references, resolving the first-100 selection defect. The client waits for the reference set to finish; a searchable selector remains a usability improvement for exceptionally large catalogs. Native query pages stay bounded.

Eight hosted queries were sampled five times each against development. All met the investigation thresholds of p95 under five seconds and response under 1 MB; observed maximum p95 was 424 ms and maximum response 18,132 bytes. These are sequential operator-to-development-network samples. Sustained production throughput/SLA requires representative approved production sizing and monitoring; no universal SLA is asserted.

Version conflicts, overreservation/movement consistency, numbering/payment/credit races, automation duplicate execution, AI stale proposals and outbox claims/retry/circuit/unknown outcomes are covered by local/native hosted tests. The 140 desktop/mobile browser cases cover actual business workflows and network-loss recovery; mobile is emulated. Controlled outage/queue tests use mocked providers to avoid sending messages or repeating accepted live provider effects.

Before an authorized production rollout, agree expected volume/concurrency, network/device targets and alerts; investigate a greater-than-2x p95 change against comparable runtime/dataset baselines. Preserve explicit capacity errors and data integrity rather than weakening validation to meet a timing target. New source/security/schema changes invalidate affected acceptance evidence.
